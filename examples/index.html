maplibre-cog-reproject examples

Reprojection of Cloud Optimized GeoTIFFs that are not in Web Mercator, for maplibre-cog-protocol.

  • Irish Grid reprojection: a COG in EPSG:29902 reprojected on the fly.
  • Vermont DEM and imagery in 3D: a DEM and 4-band imagery, each reprojected, shown in 3D.
  • Linford LiDAR: the DTM read directly compared with the DSM reprojected.
